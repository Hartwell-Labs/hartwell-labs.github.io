Security

Found something? Tell us first.

We practice coordinated disclosure across all products. Reports are acknowledged within 48 hours, fixed on a best-effort schedule, and reporters are credited in each product's Hall of Fame — with their consent.

Please avoid public disclosure before a fix is released. For sensitive details, we can provide an encrypted channel on request.
