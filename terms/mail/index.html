Service-specific terms for hosted mailboxes and webmail, supplementing the master Terms of Service.

1. Scope

These service-specific terms apply to Hartwell Mail and form part of our Terms of Service. If they conflict with the master terms, these terms prevail for Hartwell Mail.

Hartwell Mail provides hosted mailboxes, a webmail interface and, where enabled in your panel, outbound sending via our API.

2. What the service includes

The service includes mailbox storage, an inbox, a web interface and API sending where your plan enables it. Mailbox size, attachment size and daily sending limits are shown in the panel.

The service does not include IMAP, POP3 or SMTP access unless your plan or panel explicitly states otherwise. It is not a bulk mailing or e-mail marketing platform.

We may adjust limits with 14 days’ notice where a change materially worsens your plan.

3. Security and sending rules

Keep your password confidential and use at least 12 characters. Do not configure SPF, DKIM or DMARC records in a way that lets you or anyone else impersonate a domain you do not control — that is a material breach.

We may force a password reset or lock a mailbox if we reasonably suspect compromise, spam or abuse.

Prohibited uses include bulk or unsolicited mail, spoofing, phishing, malware distribution, and any activity that gets the sending domain listed on a blocklist or damages deliverability for other customers.

On a credible abuse report we may suspend sending immediately, then restore access or terminate after review.

4. Your content and privacy

Messages, attachments, contacts and settings are your Content. You keep the rights to them; we process them only to deliver, store and secure your mail.

Where we process personal data on your behalf — for example employee mailboxes — we act as processor under a data processing agreement available on request.

We do not read message content for marketing and do not use it to train models. Access for support happens on your request or to stop abuse, to the extent the law allows.

5. Backups and retention

We take backups where technically possible, but the service does not guarantee recovery of an individual message or a full mailbox. Keep copies of mail that matters to your business.

Messages you delete may persist in backups for up to 30 days, then are removed as backups rotate.

After termination, the mailbox and its contents are deleted after 30 days unless the law requires longer retention. You may request an export within 14 days of termination where technically feasible.

6. Availability and liability

The service runs on a best-effort basis without a guaranteed SLA unless your plan says otherwise.

We are not liable for lost messages caused by infrastructure provider failures, force majeure, your DNS misconfiguration, or a recipient server rejecting or filtering your mail.

Report availability issues under section 13 of the master terms within 14 days of the event.

7. Suspension and termination

We may suspend a mailbox immediately for sending-policy breaches, abuse reports, threats to infrastructure security, or payment overdue by more than 14 days.

You may request deletion at any time. Deletion is irreversible after the retention period in section 5.
